Why science fiction?

Sci-fi gives us a safe distance to ask very human questions. What do we owe each other? How much of ourselves can we trade for certainty? What happens when the future arrives before we're ready?

Why characters?

Plot tells us what happened. Character tells us why it mattered. I love tracking the small decisions, private fears and hard-won growth that make a story feel like more than spectacle.

What I'm drawn to

My favorite genres are sci-fi, thriller, psychological, action, strategy, and adventure. I'm especially drawn to Denis Villeneuve, Christopher Nolan, Hayao Miyazaki and the Wachowskis for the worlds they build and the questions they leave behind.

CineMind is an independent film journal. Every essay starts with a feeling, and follows it until it becomes a question worth sharing.
